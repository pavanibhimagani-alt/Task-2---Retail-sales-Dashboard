# Retail Store Sales Dashboard

## Overview
Interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing retail store sales data.

## Tools
. PowerBI
. Excel/CSV

## Key Insights
. Total Spending: 1.63M
. Total Quantity: 69.62k
. Total Transactions: 12.57k
. January has the highest spending.
. Online spending is higher than in-store.
. Cash has the highest spending.

## Dashboard
The dashboard shows sales trends by time, category, location, and payment method.
